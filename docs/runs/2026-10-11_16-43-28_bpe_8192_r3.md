## Observations

At the same ~81.92M sampled source-byte budget and unchanged 64,713,344 parameters, increasing recurrent passes from two to three improved best/final BPB from 0.6519 / 0.6523 to 0.6419 / 0.6409. Training-step time increased from 705.49s to 1,028.57s (~45.8%), and peak allocated memory increased from 4,562.4 to 5,957.1 MiB (~30.6%). Greedy generation throughput fell from 122.54 to 87.26 decoded UTF-8 bytes/sec (~28.8%). Reusing weights adds computation and training activations without adding parameters.

Both generation invocations restore the best checkpoint at step 2,440; final validation evaluates the last model at step 2,441, so its BPB is not the generation checkpoint's score. Periodic and final evaluations use different random validation samples and batch counts. These single-seed results do not establish statistical significance.

Sampling at temperature 0.7 and top-p 0.9 produces more varied continuations in these samples, but still repeats phrases, shifts characters and restarts stories. Greedy decoding has pronounced loops in the box and park samples. Lower BPB and the extra recurrent pass do not eliminate these generation issues. Sampling throughput was 83.88 decoded UTF-8 bytes/sec versus 87.26 for greedy; token counts and generated text differ.
