The [earlier 10,000-step 4,096 run](2026-10-10_18-42-24_bpe_4096.md) processed approximately 322.36M source bytes and achieved 0.5577 / 0.5590 best/final BPB. Its roughly 3.94 times larger budget explains why it is kept separate from the matched-budget comparison. Both runs used the same checkpoint filenames; these logs do not establish that the earlier checkpoint files were retained.

The byte baseline's best token loss of 0.5583 corresponds to approximately 0.8055 BPB (derived from rounded logged loss). Compare BPB across tokenizers, rather than token cross-entropy. BPE changes source-byte context coverage and vocabulary parameters; random token-window validation samples also differ. These are single-seed sampled validation measurements.

All five greedy samples exhibit repetition and/or restart with another story. Lower validation BPB does not eliminate these generation issues.
